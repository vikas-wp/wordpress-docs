Category Management & Mapping

Before publishing products or synchronizing catalog inventory, you must import eBay categories and link them to your WooCommerce product taxonomy.

1. Category Management (tab=manage-category)

The Categories tab displays all categories imported from your eBay seller store or eBay marketplace site.

Importing eBay Categories

  1. Open WC Ebay Connector → Manage Account → Categories.
  2. Click the Import Categories button.
  3. The connector communicates with the eBay Taxonomy API to retrieve leaf categories, parent levels, and store categories.
  4. Imported categories are indexed in the local database with their unique eBay Category ID, name, parent path, and leaf indicator.

2. Interactive Category Mapping (tab=manage-category-mapping)

The Map Categories tab allows you to establish 1-to-1 relationships between WooCommerce categories and eBay categories.

How Category Mapping Works

  1. Click Map Category to open the interactive mapping dialog.
  2. Select the WooCommerce Category from the dropdown list.
  3. Search for and select the corresponding eBay Category by browsing through eBay's category taxonomy hierarchy.
  4. When names are identical, use the Auto Map feature to pair categories automatically.

3. Configuring Item Specifics

Certain eBay categories require specific item attributes (such as Brand, Size, Color, Material, MPN, or ISBN) before a product can be accepted for listing.

  • Once an eBay category is mapped, click Configure Specifics.
  • Map each required eBay attribute to a WooCommerce product attribute taxonomy, custom field, or fixed default value.
  • When products assigned to this category are exported, the connector automatically resolves these attributes and injects them into the eBay listing payload.
