Order Lifecycle

A kitchen ticket is its own record with its own number, separate from the WooCommerce order. Knowing how the two relate saves a lot of confusion at the counter.

The five statuses

StatusWhere it appearsMoved on by
On HoldKitchen On Hold column, POS Kitchen Orders tabThe kitchen selecting Start
PreparingKitchen Preparing column, customer screen Preparing listThe kitchen selecting Mark as Completed
CompletedKitchen Completed column, customer screen Completed listThe kitchen selecting Send
SentPOS Sent Orders tab. The ticket has left the kitchen boardThe counter selecting Create Bill, or Back to Hold
DeletedNowhere. The ticket is goneThe counter selecting Delete on the Kitchen Orders tab

Every step can be walked back:

  • Preparing → On Hold with Back to Hold on the kitchen board;
  • Completed → Preparing with Back to Prepare on the kitchen board;
  • Sent → On Hold with Back to Hold on the POS Sent Orders tab.

Dine in and take away take different routes

Dine inTake away
Sent to the kitchenBefore payment, from the cartAfter payment, from the payment page
WooCommerce order at that momentDoes not exist yetAlready created
Ticket card showsDine In plus the table name and booking numberTake Away plus the WooCommerce order number
Bill raisedLater, with Create Bill on the Sent Orders tabAlready paid
More items can be addedYes, with ADD MORENo

Ticket numbers

Every ticket gets a short kitchen number that is easy to call across a kitchen. How it counts depends on the live channel.

  • Firebase numbers tickets per day. The number restarts each morning and begins with the day of the month, so on the 7th the first ticket of the day is #71, then #72, #73 and so on.
  • Socket keeps one running number that never restarts. The very first ticket is numbered the same way — the day of the month followed by 1, so #211 if the channel is first used on the 21st — and every ticket after that adds one, whatever the date: #212, #213 and onwards.

On both channels one counter is shared by all outlets.

A take away ticket also carries the WooCommerce order number on its card, shown as Take Away #219. That is the number to use when looking the order up under WooCommerce → Orders. A dine in ticket has no WooCommerce number until the bill is raised.

If you use an order number prefix

The Order Id Prefix set under Restaurant POS → Settings → General is applied to the numbers shown on the kitchen tabs inside the Point of Sale and on the printed kitchen and thermal receipts, so a ticket reads #POS_76 there. The standalone kitchen board shows the bare number, #76, and its search box matches the bare number too. Type the digits without the prefix when you look a ticket up on the board.

Does the board clear itself?

Only on the Firebase channel. Whichever channel you use, the kitchen record is not a sales history: your sales history is in WooCommerce → Orders and in the Point of Sale reports, and is never affected.

Firebase: the board resets every day

The kitchen board holds the current day only. The first time any screen connects on a new day, the previous day's tickets are cleared automatically and the ticket counter starts again.

This means:

  • the board never fills up with yesterday's work;
  • a ticket left in Completed overnight will not be there in the morning.

Important

On Firebase, finish the day's tickets before closing. Anything still on the board when the next day starts is removed, and there is no way to bring it back.

Socket: nothing is cleared automatically

On the Socket channel tickets are stored in your WordPress database and the module never removes them by date. This means:

  • a ticket left in On Hold, Preparing or Completed is still on the board the next morning, until the kitchen moves it on;
  • every ticket that has been sent stays listed on the POS Sent Orders tab;
  • a ticket is removed only when the counter deletes it with Delete on the Kitchen Orders tab. To remove a ticket from Sent Orders, select Back to Hold and then Delete.

Tips

On Socket, end each service with an empty board — send what was served and delete what was not — so the next shift starts clean.

What is written to the WooCommerce order

For a take away order sent through the payment page checkbox, WooCommerce records that the order was sent to the kitchen. Nothing else about the order — its status, its totals, its items — is changed by this module. Order statuses, stock and reports behave exactly as they do without it.

No emails

This module sends no WooCommerce email. There is no kitchen email template and no customer email about preparation. The alerts it does send are ordinary browser notifications on the kitchen board and on the POS screen, and only to a browser where the person allowed notifications.
